Before you contact anyone

  • In BC the security deposit can't be more than half a month's rent (Residential Tenancy Act).
  • Never pay a deposit or "application fee" before you've seen the unit.
  • Walk away if the landlord is "out of the country" or can't show the place.
  • No e-transfers to third parties, gift cards or crypto.
  • Reverse-search the photos. Scammers reuse them from real listings.
  • Insist on a written tenancy agreement, and don't let anyone rush you.
